Why need another app besides QuickField:
(1) QuickField is a general-purpose engineering FEM package, we need a focused educational physics app.
    1.1) QuickField is aimed mainly at engineers/researchers, we target students learning circuits and E&M.
    1.2) QuickField requires defining geometry, materials, boundary conditions, meshing, and solver settings, we want to hide those details.
    1.3) QuickField follows a model -> solve -> postprocess workflow; we want changing the wireshape and immediately see the physics update.
    1.4) QuickField can visualize many quantities for many problems; we want to focus on surface charge -> E field -> current direction.
    1.5) QuickField answers "what is the numerical field solution"; we answer "why does current know how to turn the wire".
(2) QuickField is windows desktop software; we want a lightweight and browser-based app.
